# Media provenance

## Current presentation

The page uses twelve distinct illustrative architectural photographs, one for each photographic placement. They were generated with the built-in image_gen tool for this redesign. They are concept imagery, not verified photographs of a property for sale.

Each asset has a desktop WebP rendition in `public/images/desktop/` and a smaller WebP rendition in `public/images/mobile/`. Desktop files are 1536 × 1024; the first four mobile files are 768 × 960, and the eight additional mobile files are 900 × 600. The responsive component requests the rendition appropriate to the viewport, and the image frame determines its visible crop.

All original raster images and both old MP4 files were removed at the user's request. The source ZIP and obsolete generation scripts were removed previously. No video or frame sequence is loaded.

## Image direction

- **Exterior:** pale limestone façades, planted balconies, natural glazing and landscaped approach.
- **Courtyard:** oak and stone entrance, reflecting pool and Japanese maple.
- **Living:** linen seating, walnut furniture, warm daylight and a wooded outlook.
- **Terrace:** planted rooftop, timber pergola and a view across wooded hills.
- **One-bedroom:** intimate bedroom, linen bedding, oak desk and garden views (`luma-one-bedroom`).
- **Two-bedroom:** apartment dining room, oak table and open kitchen (`luma-two-bedroom`).
- **Three-bedroom:** family living and dining spaces opening onto planted balconies (`luma-three-bedroom`).
- **Materials:** limestone, oak joinery and soft daylight (`luma-materials`).
- **Rooftop:** outdoor seating and planted beds beneath a pergola at sunset (`luma-rooftop`).
- **Wellness:** an indoor pool lined with warm limestone and oak (`luma-wellness`).
- **Garden:** native grasses and mature trees around a winding path (`luma-garden`).
- **Lounge:** sculptural armchairs, a walnut library and garden views (`luma-lounge`).

The first four assets are `luma-exterior`, `luma-courtyard`, `luma-living`, and `luma-terrace`. The visible footer states that the presentation is conceptual and that layouts and amenities remain subject to confirmation.
